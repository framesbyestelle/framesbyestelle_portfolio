About

Estelle Huy

I'm Estelle, a freelance photographer and creative based in Montreal. I've been documenting events, people, and moments since 2020, and I co-founded PPU a photography service at McGill, which has covered over 100+ campus events over the past 3 years.

Outside photography, I recently graduated in Mechanical Engineering, I hold a Private Pilot Licence, and I run a YouTube channel (framesbyestelle) exploring creativity, travel, and life as a young creative professional.

I'm always open to new collaborations. Reach out if you'd like to work together.
