About Estelle

Documenting moments
worth remembering.

Freelance photographer based in Montreal. I started out in digital editing and Photoshop, but running a campus photography service over the past five years is where I truly fell in love with being behind the camera for events and portraits. My focus is always on capturing real, unscripted moments so people can focus on being present instead of on their phones. Feel free to reach out if you'd like to work together!
